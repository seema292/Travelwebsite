Policies & Important Information

Government ID Requirements

  • Every traveler must carry an original government photo ID (Aadhaar, Passport, Voter ID or Driving License) matching the name registered on the permit.
  • Foreign nationals must carry their original passport and valid visa at the forest checkpost.
  • Permit names are strictly non-transferable; the ID is verified at the gate before entry.
  • Children's details and age must be declared at the time of booking.

Permit Cancellation Policy

Forest Department safari permits, once issued, are non-refundable and non-transferable as per government regulation. For package components (resort stays, transfers), the following applies:

  • Cancellation 15+ days before travel: 75% refund of package amount (excluding issued permits).
  • Cancellation 7–14 days before travel: 50% refund of package amount.
  • Cancellation within 7 days or no-show: no refund.
  • Safari re-scheduling is subject to live Forest Department quota availability.

Privacy & Data Security

We collect only the information needed to process your safari permits and bookings — name, contact details and government ID numbers as mandated by the Forest Department. Your data is:

  • Transmitted over 256-bit SSL encrypted connections.
  • Never sold or shared with third parties for marketing.
  • Shared with Forest Department authorities only as required for permit issuance.
  • Retained only as long as legally required, then securely deleted.

Terms of Facilitation

Wild Path Safari is a private travel facilitation and wildlife consultancy enterprise. We are not the official government website or Forest Department portal of any national park. We act as an independent on-ground facilitator for permit acquisition, verified 4x4 Gypsy allocation, naturalist arrangement and lodge stays.

  • Permit allotment, zone and vehicle assignment are decided solely by the Forest Department's computerised roster.
  • Safari timings and park closures follow official Forest Department schedules and may change without notice.
  • Wildlife sightings are subject to natural conditions and are never guaranteed.
  • By booking, you agree to abide by all forest regulations: no plastic, no loud music, no alighting from vehicles in protected zones.
