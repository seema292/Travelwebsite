About the Reserve

Gujarat's Most Celebrated Tiger Reserve — Where the Wild Still Rules

Established 1955Maharashtra's oldest national park
625.4 sq kmCore and buffer wilderness
Premier Tiger HabitatRenowned wildlife encounters

Tadoba Andhari Tiger Reserve (TAAAATR) is Maharashtra's oldest and most famous tiger reserve located in Chandrapur district. Known for outstanding Bengal tiger sightings and rich biodiversity, Tadoba National Park attracts thousands of wildlife lovers every year.

The word "Tadoba" comes from the tribal god "Taru" or "Tadoba," worshipped by local tribal communities. According to local legends, Taru was a village chief killed by a tiger and later worshipped as a deity. A shrine dedicated to Tadoba still exists near Tadoba Lake.

Hunting was banned in 1935, it was declared a National Park in 1955, and the Andhari Wildlife Sanctuary was established in 1986. In 1995 they were combined to form the Tadoba Andhari Tiger Reserve — one of Central India's finest wildlife destinations.

Best Time to Visit

When to Visit Gir National Park & Wildlife Sanctuary

Every season offers a different perspective of Tadoba. Choose warmer months for stronger tiger opportunities or winter for comfortable family safaris and birdwatching.

Best for Tiger Sightings
Summer Season
March – June
Best tiger spotting opportunities. Animals concentrate near Tadoba Lake and water bodies. Vegetation thins for better visibility. Ideal for photographers and tiger enthusiasts.
Best for Families
Winter Season
October – February
Pleasant weather and comfortable safari experience. Excellent for families and birdwatching. Migratory birds arrive. Comfortable 10–25°C temperatures. Peak tourist season.
Closed Season
Monsoon Season
July – September
Park remains closed during monsoon for wildlife breeding. Heavy rainfall and lush greenery. Buffer zones may have restricted access. Reserve reopens October 16.
March–June: Tiger focus October–February: Family comfort July–September: Core park closed
